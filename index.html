★ About FFAF ★

Funeral for a Friend are a Welsh post-hardcore band from Bridgend, formed in 2001. Their debut album Casually Dressed & Deep in Conversation became one of the defining UK post-hardcore albums of the 2000s. Since then the band have released several acclaimed records, reunited after their farewell, and continue performing live with vocalist Lucas Woodland.
